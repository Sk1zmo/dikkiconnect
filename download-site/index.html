What the app asks for, and why

Location

Sets the pickup point for a door-to-door parcel and shows where your delivery or ride is. Asked the first time you tap "Use my location" or open a live map. Only while the app is open — there is no background tracking. Decline it and you can still type addresses.

Camera

Scans the parcel QR at a hub and photographs the parcel at each handover. Those photos are what protect both sides if a delivery is ever disputed. Asked the first time you open the scanner.

Photos

Attaches an existing picture to a damage report, and saves delivery proof to your gallery when you ask it to. Asked only when you pick or save an image.

Network

Maps, sign-in codes and live tracking all need a connection. The app caches map tiles so a cold start does not re-download the whole city. Granted at install.

Worth knowing

Which one should I install? The demo, if you are showing the app to somebody or trying it yourself — it needs nothing set up. The full version, if you want a real account that persists and is shared with the people you deliver to. The demo carries a permanent badge across the top so the two can never be confused, and that badge is compiled into the demo build — it does not exist in the full one.

Signing in. DikkiConnect uses your email address and sends a six-digit code, so there is no password to remember or lose. Your mobile number is asked for at the same time, but only so a driver can reach you at the door: no code is ever sent to it.

Why Android warns you. This build is debug-signed, which is exactly what lets you install it directly rather than through the Play Store. A Play Store release needs a signing keystore held under your own developer account — that keystore permanently controls who can ship updates, so it should be generated by you and never handed over.

Not connected yet. UPI settlement confirmation and Aadhaar KYC each need commercial onboarding under a registered company — a payment gateway account and a licensed KYC partner. Both are already wired to adapters in the app, so supplying the credentials switches them on without further code changes.
